# Calculator Formulas and Scoring Criteria

## CHA₂DS₂-VASc Score

**Purpose:** Stroke risk stratification in atrial fibrillation

**Scoring:**
- **C** - CHF history: +1
- **H** - Hypertension history: +1
- **A₂** - Age ≥75: +2
- **D** - Diabetes: +1
- **S₂** - Stroke/TIA/thromboembolism history: +2
- **V** - Vascular disease (MI, PAD, aortic plaque): +1
- **A** - Age 65-74: +1
- **Sc** - Sex category (Female): +1

**Maximum Score:** 9 points

**Interpretation:**
- Score 0 (male) or 1 (female): No anticoagulation recommended
- Score 1 (male) or 2 (female): Consider anticoagulation based on patient preferences
- Score ≥2 (male) or ≥3 (female): Anticoagulation recommended

**Source:** MDCalc, Lip GY et al. Chest 2010

## HAS-BLED Score

**Purpose:** Bleeding risk assessment in patients on anticoagulation for atrial fibrillation

**Scoring:**
- **H** - Hypertension (uncontrolled, >160 mmHg systolic): +1
- **A** - Abnormal renal function (dialysis, transplant, Cr >2.26 mg/dL): +1
- **A** - Abnormal liver function (cirrhosis or Bilirubin >2x normal or AST/ALT/AP >3x normal): +1
- **S** - Stroke history: +1
- **B** - Bleeding history or predisposition: +1
- **L** - Labile INR (unstable/high INRs, time in therapeutic range <60%): +1
- **E** - Elderly (age >65): +1
- **D** - Drugs (antiplatelet agents, NSAIDs) or alcohol (≥8 drinks/week): +1 or +1

**Maximum Score:** 9 points

**Interpretation:**
- Score 0-2: Low bleeding risk (1.13-1.02% per year)
- Score ≥3: High bleeding risk (>3.74% per year)

**Note:** High bleeding risk does not contraindicate anticoagulation; it indicates need for closer monitoring

**Source:** MDCalc, Pisters R et al. Chest 2010

## TIMI Risk Score (ACS)

**Purpose:** Risk stratification for acute coronary syndrome

**Scoring (7 criteria, 1 point each):**
1. Age ≥65
2. ≥3 CAD risk factors (family history, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, diabetes, current smoker)
3. Known CAD (stenosis ≥50%)
4. Aspirin use in past 7 days
5. Severe angina (≥2 episodes in 24 hours)
6. ST changes ≥0.5mm
7. Positive cardiac biomarkers

**Maximum Score:** 7 points

**Interpretation (14-day risk of death, MI, or urgent revascularization):**
- Score 0-1: 4.7% risk
- Score 2: 8.3% risk
- Score 3: 13.2% risk
- Score 4: 19.9% risk
- Score 5: 26.2% risk
- Score 6-7: 40.9% risk

**Source:** MDCalc, Antman EM et al. JAMA 2000

## Framingham Risk Score

**Purpose:** 10-year cardiovascular disease risk prediction

**Inputs:**
- Age
- Sex
- Total cholesterol
- HDL cholesterol
- Systolic blood pressure
- Treatment for hypertension (yes/no)
- Smoking status
- Diabetes status

**Calculation:** Complex point-based system with sex-specific scoring

**Interpretation:**
- <10%: Low risk
- 10-20%: Intermediate risk
- >20%: High risk

**Source:** Wilson PW et al. Circulation 1998

## ASCVD Risk Calculator

**Purpose:** 10-year atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease risk

**Inputs:**
- Age (40-79)
- Sex
- Race (White/African American)
- Total cholesterol
- HDL cholesterol
- Systolic blood pressure
- Treatment for hypertension
- Diabetes
- Smoking status

**Calculation:** Pooled Cohort Equations (race and sex-specific)

**Interpretation:**
- <5%: Low risk
- 5-7.5%: Borderline risk
- 7.5-20%: Intermediate risk
- >20%: High risk

**Source:** Goff DC et al. Circulation 2014
